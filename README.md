# Suteja — Personal Portfolio

A personal portfolio website showcasing my work across **frontend development, UI/UX design, graphic design, and visual identity**.

Built to present not only what I create, but also how I approach digital products — combining thoughtful design with clean, maintainable frontend code.

## ✦ About

I'm a **frontend developer and designer based in Indonesia**, working across:

* Frontend Development
* UI/UX Design
* Graphic Design
* Logo & Visual Identity
* Digital Product Design

For projects that require a complete product beyond frontend and design, I also work with a team of **backend and mobile developers**.
